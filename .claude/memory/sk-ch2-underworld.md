Chapter II Underworld (Devilites / fiends / dark gods). Epigraph: `...an alternative approach would be required for bringing them back.`

**Themes:** hell reimagined as a corporate-bureaucratic underworld (Devilites as office workforce, Pit Bosses, paperwork, Undercorp at the frontier); fiendish cults worshipping elemental "dark gods" (a magma/fire deity later identified as Vog, a vile/venom deity); souls-and-energy as the realm's scarce resource; graveyards as a liminal "world between worlds."

**Motifs:** stone-cracked mineral weapons; pink = curse, native to the Underworld; iron branding and punishment (Firebranders); masks of a fiery deity; green essence as wrongness/death/ectoplasm; the evil eye beginning to appear.

**Raw description:** The mundane-occult register — hell rendered as a disgruntled white-collar bureaucracy that is also a genuine cosmic-horror conspiracy front. Establishes the Underworld as the persistent, scheming antagonist whose curse and eye-symbolism will recur in every later epoch.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `The office minions of the Underworld are a disgruntled lot`; *copious amounts of paperwork*; *dark gods*; *fiery deity of martial prowess*; graveyards as a "*world between worlds where lost souls gather, hungry*"; *vile essence*.

Expands into [[sk-ch6-undercorp-apocrea]]; curse threads into [[sk-ch8s-curse]]; descends from cosmology in [[sk-ch1-cosmology]].
