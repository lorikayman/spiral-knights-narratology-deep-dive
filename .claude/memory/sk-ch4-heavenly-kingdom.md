Chapter IV Heavenly Kingdom (Valkyries / celestial beings; Owlites as descendants). Epigraph: `It retains many divine properties celestial beings possess but lacks their weakness to fire.`

**Themes:** divine/celestial guardianship; a kingdom hidden in the sky atop snow/magma/wind-swept mountains (a Valhalla/Asgard stand-in); perpetual war with the Underworld; an avian lineage (griffin/owl); a guardian civilization that protects an entrance to paradise; celestial beings' characteristic weakness to fire; an Olympian/pantheon parallel.

**Motifs:** the spiral on Valkyrie helms; golden radiant light piercing Underworld darkness; gun-puppy stone-dragon statues that guard eternally after their makers cease; floating castles in the void; brilliant white armor; Asgard built by giants; howlitzers as undead drakes/wyverns; the dread seal / evil eye on captured gear.

**Raw description:** The "divinity, half-erased by time" lens. Heavenly kingdom is the first guardian civilization, its splendor visible only through deteriorated castles and obfuscated relics; its fall sets up the recurring tragedy where each guardian people is undone while protecting the sleeping deity.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `A helm worn by heavenly guardians who watch over a kingdom hidden atop a snow-covered mountain`; `It retains many divine properties celestial beings possess but lacks their weakness to fire`; *one who defied the gods and stormed/razed the gates of a heavenly kingdom*; *the eternal echoes of her siege*; `a priceless helmet whose name denizens of the Underworld dare not mention`; *brilliant, white helm belonging to a heavenly warrior*.

Continues into [[sk-ch7s-gunslinger-priests]] and [[sk-ch8-kat-tribe]] (Owlites); houses the Sleeper's paradise that becomes Whitespace in [[sk-ch9s-swarm]].
