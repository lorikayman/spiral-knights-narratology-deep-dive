Chapter VII-S Gunslinger Priests (weird-west priesthood; Valkyrian->Owlite transition; griffons; Fallen Valkyrian; War in Heaven). Epigraph: `...bullets serve as ushers for those that refuse to leave this world.`

**Themes:** weird-west holy gunslingers hunting the undead/Underworld in a "now forgotten time"; divine light vs Underworld darkness (Sun Silver / Luminite / Celestial Ore radiance); a civilizational shift from Valkyrian myth-divinity to Owlite *scientific* magic (myth -> reason); griffons as celestial dragon-descendants and heaven's beasts; the Fallen Valkyrian as a Lucifer/Morningstar figure cast from paradise; the War in Heaven (Revelation 12, angelic rebellion); the Celestial Kingdom as an Axis Mundi / Kunlun atop the world.

**Motifs:** the revolver-and-rosary; rawhide and gunsmoke; a golden frame/lantern of light in impenetrable dark; the halo; the griffin as beauty hiding a vicious sting; solar divinity; the angel cast down; the sealing of the Legion of Ur.

**Raw description:** A short, tonally distinct interlude — frontier-gothic holy violence bridging the kingdom's myth-age and the Owlites' reason-age. It introduces the "fallen divine" template (an angel turned fiend) that later recurs in cursed kings and the Architect.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `bullets serve as ushers for those that refuse to leave this world`; *gunslinger priests during a now forgotten time*; `they smell of rawhide, gun smoke and action`; *golden frame was believed to provide light within the impenetrable darkness of the Underworld*; `A cursed suit of armor once worn by a celestial being cast down from paradise`; *a delicate thing of beauty that hides a vicious sting*; *righteous vengeance*.

Bridges [[sk-ch4-heavenly-kingdom]] into the Owlites of [[sk-ch8-kat-tribe]]; the cast-down-angel motif feeds [[sk-ch7-almire]] and [[sk-ch9s-swarm]].
