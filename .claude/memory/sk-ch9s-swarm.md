Chapter IX-S Swarm (Herex's treason; the Void; Whitespace; the Sleeper; the Architect's madness). Epigraph: `Tear down every wall and cog in this accursed place and you will find only shadows.` The metaphysical climax; the document's title comes from here.

**Themes:** the Swarm as an eldritch consumer that devours endlessly and mirrors itself to infinity — "NEITHER BEAST NOR GOD, IT IS A CREATION UNLIKE ALL"; born when a Whitespace-entity (which helped build Clockworks) is *cursed* and "opens the Void," a botched alchemical Great Work (massa confusa / nigredo with no refinement) fusing divine Whitespace with primordial Void; the **Sleeper** as a dreaming, benevolent deity at the Core's heart — vulnerable in slumber, worlds drawn to shield it, now imprisoned and weakening as the Swarm feeds; the **Architect** (= King Tinkinzar) who struck a *Pact* with the Sleeper then "in his madness betrayed" it, twisting the fortress into a prison/game of death (madness traced to Apocrea); **Herex** as the present echo of that madness, hearing "the Truth that lies below" and the "Resonance," aligning with the Swarm; the Iron Law = the Pact = keep the Core sealed; dream-metaphysics, mirroring, and the escalation motif (each Underworld siege grows in scope: woods -> kingdom -> heaven -> whole planet).

**Motifs:** the thousand watching eyes; the maw/belly-of-the-beast descent; pink-and-black void monsters (cursed innards); the Void as deep water/abyss; the resonant heartbeat of the Core; the failed rebis (union of opposites corrupted).

**Raw description:** Cosmic horror as the secret heart of the whole timeline — the megastructure's own god-power, cursed by the Underworld, becomes a self-mirroring hunger that imprisons its maker's god. The most abstract, dread-saturated chapter; resolves the alchemy into a tragic failed transmutation.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `Tear down every wall and cog in this accursed place and you will find only shadows`; `I am the thousand eyes that watch you in the darkness. I am the hissing blade that whispers your doom`; `AS I DREAMED, IT CONSUMED. IT DEVOURED THE KNOWLEDGE OF TIME... MEMORIES OF THE DEAD... THE HEARTS OF KINGS AND THE SOULS OF THEIR PEOPLE... GOOD AND EVIL... IT CONSUMED`; `ENDLESSLY MIRRORING... EXPANDING... INFINITE`; `NEITHER BEAST NOR GOD, IT IS A CREATION UNLIKE ALL. IT IS... THE SWARM`; `WITHIN MY DREAM I SAW IT OPEN THE VOID, FELT ITS ENDLESS HUNGER`; `WITHIN MY DREAM I SEE YOUR LEGION FALLING TO THIS WORLD LIKE BURNING STARS`; `VULNERABLE IN SLUMBER, WORLDS WERE DRAWN TO SHIELD ME FROM THE DARKNESS`; "*The Swarm keeps growing stronger while The Sleeper grows weaker*."
