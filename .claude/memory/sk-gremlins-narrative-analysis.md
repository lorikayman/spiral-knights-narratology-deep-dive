Narrative-analysis (craft layer) profile of Gremlins, deliberately kept separate from the in-Universe lore in [[sk-ch9-gremlin-empire]]. Source: `src/lib/burning_stars.md` Final Chapter, section "A Metamodern Gremlin of Cradle." Companion artifact: repo-root `gremlins-influence.md` (a typed, layered Mermaid influence map) which revises the earlier `gremlins.drawio` draft.

**Framing principle:** the author analyses Gremlins twice — as a *character in the setting* and as a *standalone species* — along narrative-analysis axes: Doylist inspiration, design/body, role in the story, role in the setting, and theme. The influence map puts the species at the hub and sorts everything else into those five layers, marking where authorial inspiration crosses into in-fiction fact.

**Identity / role in story:** present civilization and first major threat on Cradle (tutorial; Razwog); *comic-relief villains* whose episodic failure to be an actualized threat conceals the real endgame (Herex -> Swarm -> Core); tone shifts grim after King of Ashes / Built to Destroy (tier 2 -> tier 3). This comic vs serious tension is the **metamodern oscillation** that titles the chapter.

**Design (Doylist genealogy):** fae folk of folklore -> wartime aircraft "Gremlin" (cause of malfunction; part of the unknown) -> Dahl's *The Gremlins* (1943; forest habitat removed, settle in machinery) -> *Gremlins* (1984) film, where **mogwai define anatomy** (fur) and the **movie Gremlin defines character** (digitigrade locomotion, mischief, vermin-like). Spiral Knights synthesises these: subverting "malfunction" into "builder (positive but haphazard)" and upscaling the habitat from aircraft/factory to the megastructure; eldritch/magitech is injected to justify the operational megastructure.

**Body & motifs:** animalistic (fur-bearing carnivore, sharp/jagged, digitigrade), raspy "vermin" voice, slim cloth-accented industrial uniform, and **goggles as the defining feature** — slitted lens = mischief (friendly/Emberlight), circular lens = horror (Void/Darkfang/possessed Groat/tier-3 purple, perhaps foreshadowing the Swarm); concealed eyes = the unknown / unpredictable intent (spectrum: Seerus's visible eyes <-> goggles <-> Strangers/Haven masks Punch & Vise). Recurring motifs: technology/malfunction subverted; industrialism and megastructure-as-home; militarism (Iron Law, Crimson Order, imperial motif); failing empire / loss of control / fall from grace (Early Empire white-blue-yellow vs Crimson red-white-black); the philosopher-king/Architect; horror re-emerging from animalistic features; weakness to shadow damage as "darkness interfering with the conscious mind" (Jungian/alchemical).

**Themes (convergence):** *metamodern oscillation* (modern enthusiasm vs postmodern irony, per Vermeulen & van den Akker); *humanity of Gremlins* (megastructure + struggle render them human despite the most animalistic body of any relatable civilization; contrast of the alien and the familiar); *industrialization self-critique* (they drive the very megastructure that critiques industrialization = a self-conscious warning); *horror re-emergence* bridging to the cosmic-horror endgame. Author's thesis: Gremlins' character is "engineered from the contrast and exploration of complex technology being wielded by a species of highly animalistic, semi-comedic and militaristic design." The Gremlin <-> megastructure bond is explicitly bi-directional.

**Draft-vs-revision note:** the `gremlins.drawio` draft floated the fall-from-grace cluster free of the species, mixed real-world referents into in-fiction outcomes without a signpost, and overloaded its edge labels (Provides/Contains/Contributes). It omitted the metamodern spine, the goggles split, Herex/loss-of-control, and the bi-directional bond. It is iterated upon in `metamodern_gremlin_of_cradle.md`, rendering gremlins as a central hub, five typed layers, and a horror throughline.
