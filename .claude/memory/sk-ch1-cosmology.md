Chapter I Cosmology (realms / energy / alchemy / dragons). Epigraph: `Since its original discovery, Dark Matter has been put to a variety of uses, from powering Spiral technology, to rearranging Gremlins on a subatomic level.`

**Themes:** a stacked cosmology of realms (Nameless, paradise/Spirit Mother's, Material, Underworld, Blinding Abyss, Apocrean, the Void); a single omnipresent *energy* as universal medium that manifests as mist/crystal energy, minerals, souls, auras, heat; *alchemy as the in-Universe metaphysics* (transmutation, "inherent power"); dragons as the most ancient corporeal species and cosmic progenitors of a creation myth.

**Motifs:** the dragon as Uroboros/Mercurius uniting all opposites; the elemental trinity rage / storm-divinity / frenzy (mapped to normal/shadow/etc.); "bones into mountains" (Norse Ymir); first matter / massa confusa / prima materia / round chaos seated in the Nameless realm; minerals as five-fold aspects of energy.

**Raw description:** The foundation layer. Everything later (curse, magitech, the Swarm) is a transfer of one universal energy across mediums; dragons seed the world and their slain powers let Rider-gods shape Cradle. The chapter quietly installs the alchemical grammar the whole document runs on.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game quotes + author terms): `a god who used lightning/rage/frenzy to carve whole worlds from stone`; `visage of beast who some believe created the world in its passing: its fire the sun, its bones the mountains`; the dragon as *symbol uniting all opposites*; the Void — `NEITHER BEAST NOR GOD, IT IS A CREATION UNLIKE ALL`; *endless hunger*.

Builds on [[sk-preamble-thesis]]; energy/curse mechanics recur in [[sk-ch5-spiral-order]] [[sk-ch6-undercorp-apocrea]] [[sk-ch8s-curse]] [[sk-ch9s-swarm]].
