Chapter VIII Kat Tribe (the Kataclysm; Owlite Academy of Magicks; spookats/black kats/grimalkin/Margrel; battle sprites; dread seals). Epigraph: `They carry the heaviest curse of all and mindlessly seek to devour magical auras.`

**Themes:** the hubris of forbidden knowledge culminating in apocalypse — the *Kataclysm* (Margrel opens the Book of Grey Feathers, drawing the Academy's tower into the Underworld); curse as a metaphysical exchange (black kats trade mind/literacy for raw curse power, mindlessly hungering to "devour magical auras"); siege warfare on the heavenly Academy (sleeper-cell Kat clans); auras bound to tome-glyphs; Underworld darkness vs warding-candle divine light; reincarnation of dead civilizations imprinted by the Core into battle sprites (seraphynx = pre-Kataclysm divine Kats, drakon = rage dragons, maskeraith = Underworld wraith/soul-manipulator); the eternal archetypal war Heavenly Kingdom vs Underworld; Cradle as a World Axis between paradise and Underworld; Kats as originally celestial beings (the "fallen cats").

**Motifs:** the grimalkin/wraith lurking in the dark; warding-candle embers; grey feathers; the Fu/Jiangshi talisman (dread seal); the evil eye on armor; the mask as soul-container; "angels from antiquity."

**Raw description:** The "magical hubris and catastrophe" lens — a divine cat-people who weaponize curse-knowledge and erase their own heaven, leaving only undead remnants and candle-lit ruins. The triumph-of-good-over-evil framing is shadowed by total loss.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `They carry the heaviest curse of all and mindlessly seek to devour magical auras`; *the one who opened The Book of Grey Feathers and brought about the Kataclysm*; `It brings great misfortune to anyone who dares to wear it`; `...candle for warding off the darkness, and that which lurks within it`; `These cursed wraiths cannot be destroyed and the only thing they truly fear is the light of the enchanted candles`; *the triumph of goodly magical arts over evil*; the seraphynx "*embodies divine light*"; *righteous vengeance*.

Owlites descend from [[sk-ch4-heavenly-kingdom]] / [[sk-ch7s-gunslinger-priests]]; curse is fully theorized in [[sk-ch8s-curse]]; the Kataclysm's darkness links to [[sk-ch6-undercorp-apocrea]].
