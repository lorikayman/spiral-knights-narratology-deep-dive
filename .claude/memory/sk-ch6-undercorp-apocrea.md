Chapter VI Undercorp (Underworld corporation) + Apocrea (eldritch apex). Epigraph: `Only souls keep these engines roaring, heard screaming across distant stars.`

**Themes (Undercorp):** corporate-occult hell — bureaucracy fused with ritual magic; ritual circles as alchemical "Circles of Art" / Solomonic pentacles (warding + summoning); Grim Gates / graviton tech / fiendish glyphs as a soul-and-energy economy; a minerals-and-souls universal-energy framework (Crimsonite = passion/anger, Valestone = life, Luminite = faith/wisdom/zeal, Moonstone = knowledge, Dark Matter = chaos/nothingness); the evil eye as the Underworld's omnipresent mark; curse bleeding into the material realm; the tortodrone betrayal/genocide of Tortomega.

**Themes (Apocrea):** the eldritch apex — oldest realm "as old as reality itself" (Yog-Sothothery, Lovecraft's Dreamlands: Kadath/Leng/Carcosa/King in Yellow); souls fueling dream-built "dark machines"; *negation of existence* — a god of death that denies cyclic rebirth, returning the cosmos to "primordial black"; black-and-white apex evil; madness from forbidden revelation; impossible geometry; insomnia/dreams. Plus the Strangers/Haven (nomadic dust-cloaked masked miners) and the Nefarious Imp (singular shadow antagonist).

**Raw description:** "Hell as a corporation" widening into a cosmic-horror conspiracy, with Apocrea as the true ancient horror behind it. Souls are currency and fuel; the deepest evil wants not conquest but the erasure of everything back to nothing.

**Emotional vibe pillars** (in-game + author terms): `Only souls keep these engines roaring, heard screaming across distant stars. Only souls can fill their void, sublime in its vast emptiness`; *within cities where geometry and madness share meaning*; *return this cosmic play to its primordial black*; *I weep and laugh before the shroud that waits*; Dark Matter as "*essences of both chaos and nothingness*"; *Eternal Torment Division*.
